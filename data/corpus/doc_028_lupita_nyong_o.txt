# title: Lupita Nyong'o
Lupita Amondi Nyong'o (] , ] ; born March 1, 1983) is a Kenyan-Mexican actress.  She was born in Mexico to Kenyan parents and raised in Kenya.  She attended college in the United States, earning a bachelor's degree in film and theater studies from Hampshire College.
