# title: Albanians in Montenegro
Albanians in Montenegro (, Albanian: "Shqiptarët e Malit të Zi" ) constitute 4.91% of the county's total population.  Albanians of Montenegro are Ghegs who mainly live in southeastern and eastern Montenegro, mainly in the following municipalities: Ulcinj (71% of population), Plav (19%), Bar (6%), Podgorica (5%) and Rožaje (5%).
